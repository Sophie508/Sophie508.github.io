03Understand

Close together

These belong to each other.

Far apart

These are strangers.

Evenly spaced

The space is saying nothing at all.

When two things sit near each other, I assume they’re related. When they sit far apart, I assume they aren’t. I do this before I read anything, and so does everybody else.

That means the space between things is never empty. It’s information. If a heading sits closer to the paragraph above it than to its own, I’ve told the reader something false without typing a word.

So the job is simple to say: put related things close, push unrelated things apart, and make the two distances obviously different.
