03Understand

The same

Calm. These two are equals.

Almost the same

Is that on purpose? I can’t tell.

Really different

A decision. Now one of them leads.

Contrast is how a page says “start here”. If everything is the same size, the same weight and the same colour, then nothing is more important than anything else, and the reader has to do the sorting for me.

The trap is the middle. Text at 14 next to text at 16 isn’t contrast. It’s different enough to notice and not different enough to mean anything, so it looks like I slipped.

So when two things aren’t the same, I make them really not the same. Big against small, heavy against light, dark against pale, crowded against empty.

06Make

Now go and find one.

  1. Design Sighting

    Find a page or a sign where the most important thing is not the most visible thing. Photograph it.

  2. Ten minutes

    Take one slide. Pick the single line that matters most and make it at least three times bigger than anything else. Make everything else quieter.

  3. My rule

    If it isn’t the same, don’t make it almost the same.
