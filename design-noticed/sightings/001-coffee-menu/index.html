Design Sighting #001

Coffee Menu

#Contrast

What I noticed
The stall name is enormous and the drinks are small and quiet. From across the food court I can tell what is sold here. Up close I can read the prices.
Why it works, or doesn’t
There are only two volumes, loud and quiet, and nothing in between. The big word gets me to the stall and then gets out of the way.
What I would change
I would make the prices a touch heavier than the drink names. They are the second thing I look for.

My rule

One loud thing. Everything else quiet.
