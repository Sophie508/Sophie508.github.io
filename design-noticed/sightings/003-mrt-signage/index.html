Design Sighting #003

MRT Signage

Singapore#Alignment

What I noticed
Arrows sit in one column, the coloured badges in a second, the words in a third. Every row is built on the same three lines.
Why it works, or doesn’t
I read this sign while walking. Because every word starts in the same place, my eye drops straight down the column instead of hunting row by row.

My rule

If people read it while moving, give every row the same start.
