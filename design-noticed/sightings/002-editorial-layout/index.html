Design Sighting #002

IKEA / Editorial Layout

#Repetition

What I noticed
Every product sits in the same module. A picture, a bold name, two lines of text, and a yellow price tag in the same corner.
Why it works, or doesn’t
After the first module I know where to look in all the others. The yellow tag repeats so reliably that I can scan a whole spread for prices without reading anything.

My rule

Teach the pattern once, then keep the promise.
