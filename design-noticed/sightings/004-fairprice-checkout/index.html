Design Sighting #004

FairPrice Self Checkout

Singapore#Proximity#Hierarchy

What I noticed
Payment-related information belongs conceptually together, but some instructions are visually separated.
Why it works, or doesn’t
I looked at the screen to find out how to pay, then had to search the machine for the reader. The instruction and the thing it points to are a whole screen apart.
What I would change
Move the instruction to the corner of the screen nearest the reader, and point at it.

My rule

Physical distance should reflect conceptual distance.
