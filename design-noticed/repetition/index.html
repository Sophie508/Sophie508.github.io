03Understand

Once

An accident.

Twice

A coincidence.

Three times

A decision. Now it’s a system.

Repetition means making a few decisions and then making them again, everywhere. The same typeface for every heading. The same red for every important thing. The same gap between every block.

It doesn’t mean stamping one graphic on everything. What repeats can be very quiet: a type size, a spacing, a corner, the way pictures are cropped, an edge that everything lines up to.

When enough of these repeat, separate pieces start to feel like one family, even when one is a business card and the other is a poster.

06Make

Now go and find one.

  1. Design Sighting

    Find a shop, a brand or a building where the same decision shows up at least three times: a colour, a shape, a type style. Photograph all three.

  2. Ten minutes

    Take a document with several headings. Make every heading identical: same size, same weight, same space above it. Touch nothing else.
