03Understand

A shared edge

One line ties all of these together.

Far apart

Still connected, because they share an edge.

Almost aligned

This just looks like a mistake.

When I drop things onto a page wherever there’s room, the page looks like things were dropped onto it. When each thing lines up with something else, a line appears that nobody drew, and my eye follows it.

Centring everything is the safe choice, which is why it so often looks timid. A centred block has soft, ragged sides. A hard left or right edge gives the eye something to hold.

Alignment can also do something proximity can’t. It connects things that are far apart. A date at the bottom of a poster still belongs to the picture at the top, if they start on the same line.

    06Make

    Now go and find one.

    1. Design Sighting

      Find something in public where one invisible line runs through at least three things. Photograph it, then draw the line on the photo.

    2. Ten minutes

      Take a slide where everything is centred. Pick one hard edge, left or right, and hang every single thing on it.

      Nothing is placed at random.
