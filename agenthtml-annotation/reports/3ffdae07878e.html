The task

What the annotator was asked to do

The instruction bundles six constraints: room count (2), occupancy (4 adults), hotel class (5-star), four amenities (pool, minibar, bathtub, spa), a proximity sort (nearest to the city centre), and dates (June 13–16). The recorded session satisfies some of these explicitly and others only implicitly — see Findings.

🏨

Two rooms, four adults

Two counters incremented in the rooms widget — two rooms, two adults each. Explicitly shown as “2 Rooms, 4 Adults” on the results bar.

⭐

5-star class

The Star rating filter was opened and the “5” chip engaged; the resulting results bar reported “5 filters applied” territory. Attribution is inferred — see the caveat in Findings.

🍸

Pool · Mini-bar · Bathtub · Spa

Four amenity checkboxes ticked across two different filter groups: Property facilities (Pool, Spa) and Room facilities (Mini-bar, Bathtub).

📐

Nearest to city centre

Rather than a distance radius chip, the annotator used the results sort control and picked the Distance from city centre option, then took the top-ranked hotel.

📅

13th → 16th, three nights

A date-range picker was opened, a month chosen and two day cells clicked. The month selected in the recording is Jul, not June — a real mismatch with the task text.

🏁

Ends at “Book now”

The session closes on the review/payment screen for Hyatt Regency Dubai, clicking Book now. No post-booking confirmation page is part of the recording.

Findings & uncertainty

What the recording actually shows

Stated plainly, including the parts that don't line up with the task text.

⚠ Dates are July, not June discrepancy

The task says June 13 to 16. The recorded calendar strip shows Mar–Oct with Jun present, but the annotator clicked Jul (action 04) and then 13 and 16. Every downstream snapshot reads “13 Jul 2023 – 16 Jul 2023, 3 nights”. Night count (3) and days (13→16) match the intent; the month does not.

⚠ The 5-star step is undocumented inferred

Action 12 has an empty candidate list and the action repr is just [path], so its target element is not recoverable from the data. Inference: the filter panel has a Star rating group with chips 1–5; the applied-filter counter rises from 0 to 1 across this step; and node ids in the neighbouring snapshots jump by roughly the size of one results-page render. The most plausible reading is a click on the “5” chip, but this should be treated as an inference, not a recorded fact.

✓ All four amenities are explicit verified

Pool, Mini-bar, Bathtub and Spa are individually ticked (actions 13–16) on checkbox inputs with distinct backend node ids, and each appears as checked in the following snapshot. Note they span two filter groups: Pool/Spa under Property facilities, Mini-bar/Bathtub under Room facilities.

✓ “Nearest to city centre” via sort, not radius verified

The distance-radius chips (1/5/10/15/+20 km) were left alone. Instead the sorting bar's Distance from city centre option was chosen (action 18), and the top card was then opened (action 19) — a defensible interpretation of “nearest”.

ℹ Occupancy arithmetic checks out verified

Counters start at 1 adult per room. Two “+1” clicks plus “+ Add room” yield 2 rooms × 2 adults = 4 adults, confirmed verbatim in the results bar (“2 Rooms, 4 Adults”).

ℹ Final property and price observed

The last screen is Hyatt Regency Dubai, Deira Corniche Alkhaleej Road, Dubai, with price details reading 2 Room · $612.66 and both “Pay now” and “Pay at the property” options. The hotel's star class is not asserted anywhere in the captured DOM — the 5-star constraint rests on the inferred filter plus the property's known class, not on a recorded label.

ℹ The run stops at “Book now” incomplete tail

Action 20 is the final entry. There is no confirmation, booking reference or payment-success page in the trajectory, so “completed run” here means the recorded demonstration ends on the booking click — not that a confirmation page was captured.

ℹ Observation scale context

Result-page snapshots run 159–200 KB of cleaned HTML with 997–1486 paired distractors per step, versus 45–66 KB and ~220–355 distractors on the search form. Almost all of the reporting difficulty sits in the filter and results stages.
