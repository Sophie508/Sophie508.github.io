Why these filters

Every constraint in the brief becomes a toggle.

The demonstration narrows inventory with property facilities, room amenities, property type, and distance ranking before choosing a room.

Pool

FAQ

About this case presentation

What benchmark does this case come from?
Mind2Web (upstream). Case id 05_test_task_test_task_2_4, trajectory format mind2web-json, model field human-annotator. Website field is ryanair under Travel / Airlines.
Why do dates say July instead of June?
The natural-language task mentions June 13–16, but the annotated demonstration opens the date picker, switches the month control to Jul, then selects 13 and 16. Result headers show 13 Jul 2023 – 16 Jul 2023.
How were rooms and guests configured?
Adult counter incremented for room 1, “+ Add room” pressed, adult counter incremented for room 2, then Done — producing 2 rooms and 4 adults before Search.
What was the final booked property?
After facility filters and sorting by distance from city centre, the demonstrator chose Hyatt Regency Dubai (0.93 km) via Choose room, then Book now on a room card.
Are screenshots embedded?
No local screenshots/ or frames/ were present under this case directory. The prototype reconstructs UI state from action_reprs, operations, and cleaned-html text signals. References list in case_meta was empty.
