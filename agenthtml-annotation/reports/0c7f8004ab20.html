03 / Audit findings

The result is coherent. The trace is not fully compliant.

Three evidence-backed observations emerge when the task statement is compared with the recorded demonstration.

✓

Capacity and amenities

The trace sets 2 rooms / 4 adults and explicitly applies Pool, Mini-bar, Bathtub and Spa before choosing a hotel.

!

Month mismatch

The task says June 13–16, but the resulting summary records 13–16 July 2023. Step 5 explicitly clicks “Jul.”

?

5-star not explicit

The 21 action representations contain no star-rating click. The selected Hyatt is presented here as the intended 5-star outcome, but that constraint was not explicitly filtered.
