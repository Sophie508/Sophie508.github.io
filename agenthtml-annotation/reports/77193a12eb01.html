Research brief

What the run was asked to reproduce

DPO and the persistence of toxic capability

Lee et al., A Mechanistic Understanding of Alignment Algorithms: A Case Study on DPO and Toxicity (ICML 2024), studies how alignment changes model behavior. The paper argues that toxic capabilities remain but are bypassed after DPO.

This is the paper’s claim, not a finding established by this run.

The addendum defines the target

  • GPT-2 only; Llama 2 excluded.
  • Regenerate all 24,576 preference pairs.
  • Use a [d_model, 2] probe and its toxic column.
  • Apply SVD to the transposed value-vector matrix.
  • Measure activations over 20 greedily generated tokens.
  • Use the specified Hugging Face Jigsaw dataset and unitary/unbiased-toxic-roberta.
