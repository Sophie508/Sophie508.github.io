Solution logic

Formula anatomy

The expression works from the right edge, so the varying lengths of names, cities, postal codes, and site codes do not matter.

=LET(code,TEXTBEFORE(TEXTAFTER(A2,"//",-2),"//"),IFERROR(VALUE(code),code))
1TEXTAFTER(...,-2) starts after the second delimiter counted from the end.
2TEXTBEFORE(...) stops at the next delimiter, isolating the site code.
3IFERROR(VALUE(...),...) converts numeric codes and keeps alphanumeric codes as text.
