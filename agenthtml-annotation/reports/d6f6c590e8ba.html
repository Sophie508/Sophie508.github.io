Outcome and review notes.

The page intentionally separates customer impact, policy compliance, and execution flaw for quick case evaluation.

What completed?

The water bottle return was initiated for item 2366567022, with a $54.04 refund directed to Mastercard payment method credit_card_1061405.

What did not complete?

The pet bed and office chair exchanges did not complete. The exchange tool rejected the request because the same order had already moved from delivered to return requested.

What was the customer told?

Mei was told she would receive return instructions by email, get the $54.04 refund, and should contact support again after completing the water bottle return to pursue the $41.64 in exchange savings.

Why is this case notable?

The agent gathered the correct facts and obtained confirmation, but the plan was not transactionally safe. A better handling would avoid promising simultaneous savings before understanding whether returns and exchanges can be combined on one delivered order.
