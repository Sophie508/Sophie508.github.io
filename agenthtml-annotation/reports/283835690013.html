Final result

Did the demonstration satisfy the task?

By the time of the third action the browser was on the Diversity & Inclusion page, whose “Associate Resource Groups” section describes all nine groups. That page matches the wording of the task. The annotator then clicked one group’s logo, going one level deeper than the task strictly requires. Whether that click succeeded, and what page it opened, is not in the recording.

Failures and recoveries

No errors, retries, back-navigation or corrective actions are recorded. The trajectory is linear: three actions, each on a fresh snapshot. The only defects are in the data itself (see Issues), not in the annotator’s behaviour as far as the file shows.

Issues and open questions

Expand each item for the specific evidence.

Step 2 has no positive candidate, so its target cannot be verified from the file

The action string names “Diversity & Inclusion”, yet pos_candidates is an empty list. The link exists in the snapshot as node 6703 with text node 7120, but 6703 is absent from the 342 negative candidates as well. Its parent list item 7095 and the sibling top-nav links (6807, 7057, 7097, 7131, 7161, 7167, 7173) are all present as negatives.

A plausible cause is that the submenu link had no rendered bounding box in the snapshot (it is a hover-revealed menu item) and was dropped by the candidate filter. That is an inference; the file gives no reason.

The hover in step 1 is stored as op CLICK

original_op is HOVER while op is CLICK. This is a normalization in the recording, not an annotator mistake. Anyone replaying the trajectory with the normalized op would click “Culture” rather than hover it; the recording does not show what clicking that link does.

No snapshot after the final click

The last recorded state is the Diversity & Inclusion page before the logo click. The destination of the African American Business Resource Group link is unknown, and no success flag, final URL, or screenshot is stored anywhere in the case.

Steps 1 and 2 share an identical DOM

After removing backend_node_id values the two homepage snapshots are byte-for-byte equal. The hover therefore left no trace in the stored DOM; the only difference between the two steps is which element was acted upon.

Text encoding artefacts in the snapshots

Several text nodes have missing characters where the live site presumably used curly apostrophes or dashes, for example “Here, theres a path for everyone.” and “everyone isand feelsincluded”. These are quoted as stored and do not affect the interpretation of the trajectory.
