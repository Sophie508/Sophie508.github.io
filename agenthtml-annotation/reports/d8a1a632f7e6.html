04 / Evidence & limitations

Keep the conclusion traceable.

What price and room were selected?+
The action 21 target (node 96647) is the first Book now button for “Room, 1 King Bed - 1 King.” Its offer lists free cancellation, parking and Free WiFi, with “Pay now.” Two rooms cost $612.66 plus $137.88 tax and fees, totaling $750.54. A further $32.68 local tax is payable at the property. These are recorded offer amounts, not evidence of a charge.
How certain are the filter states?+
In the action 20 cleaned HTML, Pool, Mini-bar, Bathtub, Spa and Hotels all have input_checked="true". The interface still displays “4 filters applied”; that counter alone is not a reliable count of checked inputs. Action 13 is an unlabeled path click with an empty positive-candidate list. Its exact target remains uncertain.
Does “nearest” mean nearest in all Dubai?+
The evidence supports choosing the first displayed result after “Distance from city centre” sorting. The site labels 0.93 km from “Dubai, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.” It does not establish the exact geographical centre, exhaustiveness of inventory, or real-world nearest qualifying property.
