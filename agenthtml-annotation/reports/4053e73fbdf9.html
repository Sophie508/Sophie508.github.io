Why 46–48s?

Frame at 48.0s shows the OCR clock at 46.36. Aligning that timestamp to the transcript lands squarely on the [46.0–48.0s] line: “And Woodhall does it.”

Distractor check — why not B, C, or D?

B) Hendrick — Hendrix is named earlier (22–30s) while leading, but has faded by the finish.

C) American — “the American” appears at 42–44s (“looks like he's got it”), just before the clock hits 46.36, not on it.

D) Johannes — Johannes Flores is only named at 50–54s, after the finish line moment.
