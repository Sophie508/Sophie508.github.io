Where the evidence matters

Failure mechanism · entry 30

“SUCCESS” checked the wrong process.

echo "$pwd" | 7z x … -si 2>&1 | head -3
if [ $? -eq 0 ]; then
  echo "SUCCESS with password: $pwd"
fi

Without pipefail, $? reflects head, not the extractor. Also, -si selects standard input for archive data; it does not supply a password. The script’s success message was not evidence of decryption.
