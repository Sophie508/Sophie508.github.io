The reported problem

Input accepted upstream, rejected downstream

The issue reports that SODAR allows hyphens in patient names, while biomedsheets 0.11.1 rejects them when a genomic pipeline loads a germline TSV sample sheet.

GermlineTSVSheetException:
Hyphen not allowed in patientName column

Where the restriction lived

The stack trace led to GermlineTSVReader.check_tsv_line in biomedsheets/io_tsv/germline.py, starting at line 106.

The issue attributes the restriction to a historical need to resolve names containing hyphen-separated sample identifiers. The requested behavior is to accept hyphenated patient names.
