Why it failed

Diagnosis

!Root causes

  • No seek. The natural move is to jump straight to 00:08 and snapshot. Instead the agent relied on passive playback and hoped to land on the exact second.
  • Overshoot, no recovery. Once the clock passed 00:08 to 00:10, waiting could never bring it back. The agent lacked a "I've gone too far, seek backward" branch.
  • Fragile window control. An invalid window id and a fruitless alt-tab dropped it to the desktop, forcing a full VLC relaunch that reset all progress.
  • Repetition without escalation. Six identical Agent.wait() calls in a row with no change in outcome and no plan revision.

◇Never attempted

  • Frame capture. No VLC snapshot / scene-filter action was ever issued — the core sub-goal was never executed.
  • LibreOffice Impress. The presentation, though "opened," was never focused or edited.
  • Set slide 2 background. The final and defining step of the task was never reached.
  • Verification. No check that a frame file existed or that the background applied.
