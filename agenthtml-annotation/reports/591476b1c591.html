Why the payment step failed ▾
HelloFresh embeds card capture in a cross-origin Stripe iframe. In the sandboxed environment that iframe reported a perpetual loading state; the submit button's disabled attribute stayed true. The agent tried Tab navigation, an F5 reload, and a forced click — none unblocked validation — so it went idle and the harness stopped the session.
