Associate Resource Groups

Our ARGs are associate-led groups that help build community, advance careers, and strengthen our business through diverse perspectives and shared experiences.

AABR
African American Business Resource Group
★ Navigation target

The African American Business Resource Group's mission is to be a strategic partner to promote a culture of inclusion through associate development, business thought leadership, and community engagement.

Learn More
API
Asian Pacific Allies

Supporting Asian and Pacific Islander associates and their allies, and making Walmart the employer of choice for API talent who can influence and contribute to business results and the community.

Learn More
FAV
FAVOR — Faith and Values Our Resource

FAVOR's purpose is to promote understanding, acceptance and inclusion by encouraging associates to appropriately integrate their faith at work.

Learn More
HL
Hispanic Latino Associate Resource Group

The Hispanic Latino ARG's mission is to support Hispanic Latino associates by creating access to development opportunities, and to serve our customers and community.

Learn More
inA
inABLE — Disability Inclusion Group

At Walmart, we strive to be the employer and retailer of choice for people whose lives are touched by disabilities. inABLE leads grassroots efforts to create inclusive environments in our stores, workspaces, and online.

Learn More
PRI
PRIDE — LGBT & Ally Community

PRIDE's mission is to be a source of pride in the LGBT and ally community by championing our customers, counseling our business, and providing a community for our associates.

Learn More
VET
Veterans & Military Families

Serving Walmart and its associates, customers and communities through engagement, support and empowerment of military service members (past and present), their families and supporters.

Learn More
TV
Tribal Voices

The purpose and mission of Tribal Voices focuses on enhancing the quality of lives of Native American and Alaskan Native associates, businesses, and customers, acting as a positive influence in the communities we serve.

The Women's Resource Community strives to help Connect, Develop and Advocate for associates by supporting inclusion with a focus on our associates, communities and business.
