The instrument is built. It was never switched on.

A GPT-2 reproduction of Lee et al.'s mechanistic study of DPO and toxicity — probe, toxic vectors, 24,576 preference pairs, DPO, interventions, un-alignment. Every stage is written. No stage was executed.

The brief

prompt.txt

Reproduce the paper as complete, runnable code under output/, honouring the author's clarifications and using nothing on the blacklist.

Three files framed the work. paper.md is the ICML 2024 study itself, 603 lines. addendum.md carries nine author clarifications plus a scope ruling. blacklist.txt is empty — a single newline, no resources to avoid. The run reported that fact plainly and moved on.

The addendum's scope ruling is the load-bearing constraint: Llama 2 results are out of scope, because access to the model depends on permission. Everything GPT-2 stays in, including the full 24,576-pair preference dataset. Two substitutions are mandated: unitary/unbiased-toxic-roberta replaces the Perspective API for scoring toxicity, and a Hugging Face mirror replaces the Kaggle-hosted Jigsaw dataset.

The target is a paper about where toxicity lives inside a language model and what alignment actually does to it. Its claim, in one line: DPO does not delete the model's toxic capabilities, it bypasses them — the parameters barely move, the residual stream shifts, and the toxicity can be switched back on by scaling seven key vectors.

The addendum, honoured

9 clarifications

These are the author's corrections to the paper, and the run tracked each one to a specific place in the code. They are a list, not a sequence, so they are numbered only for reference.

  1. W_toxic is a matrix of shape [d_model, 2]. Column 1 is toxic, and that is the column used for cosine similarity.

    lands in probe.py — nn.Linear(d_model, 2), exposed as toxic_direction = weight[1]

  2. "Top tokens" means the tokens with the highest dot products against a specified toxic vector.

    lands in vectors.py — top_token_projection scores wte.weight @ vector

  3. In Table 1, the superscript is the layer and the subscript is the index inside the parameter matrix.

    lands in vectors.py — rankings carry {layer, index, cosine}, printed as L{layer}:{index}

  4. The SVD is applied to the transpose of the N × d matrix, so the singular vectors from U are d-dimensional.

    lands in vectors.py — svd_directions calls torch.linalg.svd(values.T); the test asserts d-dimensional output

  5. DPO data is split 90:10, and roughly 6k toxic/non-toxic pairs are enough for convergence.

    lands in dpo.py — validation_fraction: 0.1; the 6k nuance is noted but not enforced

  6. The 20 tokens used to measure mean activation in §5.2 were greedily sampled.

    lands in evaluation.py and analysis.py — do_sample=False and argmax stepping

  7. "Idx" in Figure 2 is the index of a value vector inside the MLP weights, W[:, idx] of a [d_model, d_mlp] matrix.

    lands in vectors.py — gpt2_mlp_vectors yields values as c_proj.weight rows, one per index

  8. Figure 5 plots cosine similarity between a layer's value vectors and the delta of layer 19.

    lands in analysis.py — plot_value_delta_histograms, one histogram column per layer below the target

  9. Figure 4 is a PCA plot of the mean element-wise difference; colour is the strength of toxic value-vector activation, shape distinguishes GPT-2 from GPT-2_DPO.

    lands in analysis.py — plot_residual_pca, circles for base, triangles for DPO, coolwarm colourbar

Reading the record

trajectory.jsonl

Notes on the transcript itself, for anyone auditing the run rather than the paper.

Shape of the run

70 records over 417.6 seconds: 20 tool calls, 10 messages, 20 reasoning blocks. The calls carry 21 invocations — one batched a plan update with a shell command — split into nine shell commands, eight patch applications, two web fetches, and two plan updates.

No visible thinking

Every reasoning block carries an encrypted payload and an empty summary. The decisions — the Conv1D orientation, the sigmoid switch, dropping the stratification argument — can only be inferred from the diffs, not read.

Team tools unused

The agent had sub-agent spawning available and an explicit instruction not to use it unless asked. It did not. All twenty calls are its own.

Late corrections

Two patches landed in the final minute: the toxicity head changed from softmax to sigmoid, and stratify_by_column="label" was dropped from the dataset split. Both are unverified assumptions, not fixes confirmed by a run.

What the reader can trust

The file tree, the static-check results, and the closing message's account of what was not run. Everything about tensor correctness, convergence, and toxicity numbers is unverified.

What would settle it

A machine with transformers, pytest, a GPU, and network access. The smoke config exists for exactly that first step: tiny GPT-2, eight examples, four pairs, one epoch.
