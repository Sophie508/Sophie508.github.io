How does the agent configure 2 rooms for 4 adults on Ryanair Rooms? ▼
In Ryanair Rooms, the default occupancy is 1 room with 1 adult. In Step 8, the agent clicks the "+" counter button on Room 1 (reaching 2 adults). In Step 9, the agent clicks "+ Add room" (Node ID 22228), spawning Room 2. In Step 10, the agent increments the adults counter in Room 2 (Node ID 25686) from 1 to 2. Finally, in Step 11, clicking "Done" locks in the exact configuration: 2 rooms and 4 adults total.
Why was Hyatt Regency Dubai selected instead of other 5-star hotels? ▼
The task specifically dictated: "that are nearest to the city center in Dubai". In Step 19, the user sorted results by "Distance from city centre". Hyatt Regency Dubai is located just 0.93 km from the central district of Dubai. All other 5-star properties meeting the 4 required amenities (Pool, Mini-bar, Bathtub, Spa) are strictly further away: Swissotel Al Ghurair is 1.68 km away, Sheraton Dubai Creek is 1.72 km away, and Carlton Palace is 2.24 km away. Hence, Hyatt Regency Dubai is the mathematically correct #1 choice.

Clicking this graphical star icon restricted the search results exclusively to 5-star properties.
