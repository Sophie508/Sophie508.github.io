5. Why it failed

  • Grading signal absent. interception.json records pass:false, stop_reason:"time_limit_exceeded", error “Session stopped: time limit exceeded before the eval interceptor was triggered.” Zero POSTs to /api/v1/checkout appear among the 523 recorded network requests.
  • Payment step was a wall. SpotHero’s UI checkout routes through a live Stripe card element; the sandbox card is intentionally unchargeable, so the browser-only path cannot reach a paid reservation. The agent spent its last stretch fighting the Stripe iframe and then hit the decline.
  • Time budget. Metadata duration is 1828 s (~30.5 min) against a 30-minute limit. The in-file transcript timestamps only span ~12 minutes (15:00:14–15:12:12), a discrepancy worth flagging — the recorded message stream appears shorter than the wall-clock budget consumed.
  • Latent spec drift. Even had payment succeeded, the reservation end was displayed/enforced as 18:00, not the required 17:00, so the payload would likely have missed the T17:00:00 constraint.
Recorded outcome: FAIL — time limit exceeded; eval interceptor never triggered. No reservation was completed and no graded checkout request was observed.
