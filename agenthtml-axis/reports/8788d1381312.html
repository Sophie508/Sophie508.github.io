Robust Large Vision-Language Models via Class-Token Latent Anchoring

Foundation multi-modal models (LLaVA-1.5, OpenFlamingo) inherit the catastrophic adversarial vulnerabilities of their frozen CLIP vision towers. Under an ℓ∞ perturbation radius of merely 2/255, standard CLIP experiences a 100% hijack success rate. While supervised fine-tuning (TeCoA) prevents attacks, it shatters the zero-shot representation space (ΔL_clean = 292.7) and causes rampant hallucination. FARE resolves this dilemma by unsupervised adversarial distillation directly to the unperturbed teacher class token, delivering plug-and-play downstream LVLM robustness with zero downstream retraining.
