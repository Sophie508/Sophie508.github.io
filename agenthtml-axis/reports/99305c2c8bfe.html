The issue

A validation rule outlived its purpose.

SODAR sample sheets permit hyphens in the patient column, but biomedsheets rejected them — halting the SNAPPY genomic pipeline mid-run.

Where it broke

Snakemake aborted while loading the germline TSV sheet. The traceback lands squarely inside the reader's per-line validation.

GermlineTSVSheetException: Hyphen not allowed in patientName column ...abstract/__init__.py, line 266 _cached_read_germline_tsv_sheet io_tsv/germline.py, line 186 read_germline_tsv_sheet io_tsv/base.py, line 395 _create_sheet_json io_tsv/germline.py, line 110 check_tsv_line ← the guard

Why the rule existed

The maintainer explained the constraint was a relic — and safe to lift.

“Its origin is that I originally wanted to resolve SAMPLE-NORMALTUMORNUMBER-EXTRACTIONNUMBER-LIBRARYNUMBER unambiguously to SAMPLE. This functionality isn't actually used — it can be removed.”

So the fix isn't a workaround. It removes a guard that no longer guards anything, restoring parity with SODAR's own naming rules. Environment: biomedsheets 0.11.1.

The fix

Three checks, cleanly disabled.

The patch touches a single method in one file. The hyphen guards on patientName, fatherName and motherName are commented out; every other validation stays intact.
