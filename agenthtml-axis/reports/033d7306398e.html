FAQ

Questions about this run

Why not just exact-match the song title?

In this catalog, “Get Me Bodied” never appears as a clean title/artist pair. Entries carry compilation names, track prefixes like “022-”, and sometimes fold the artist into the title with a null artist column. Exact equality returned zero rows and a false $0.00.

Which track IDs actually contributed money?

Only 4233 ($377.62) and 12954 ($223.82). The other two fuzzy matches (#10838, #15158) had no Canada · Apple Music sales rows.

How was accent handling done?

The final SQL accepted both beyoncé and beyonce in the artist field, and also allowed the artist name to appear inside the title when the artist column was empty.
