Beyoncé filter + Apple Music Canada join answer 22:17:27 · 0.6s

Keep tracks whose artist is a Beyoncé variant or whose title embeds her name, then SUM revenue for Canada × Apple Music per track_id.

filter logicpython + duckdb
tracks WHERE lower(title) LIKE '%get me bodied%'
  AND (
    lower(coalesce(artist,'')) IN ('beyoncé','beyonce')
    OR lower(title) LIKE '%beyoncé%'
    OR lower(title) LIKE '%beyonce%'
  )

sales WHERE country='Canada' AND store='Apple Music'
  AND track_id IN (...)
GROUP BY track_id
