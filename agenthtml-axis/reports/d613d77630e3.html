catalog messiness

Four catalog IDs refer to the same song under slightly different strings. Only two of them have Canada/Apple Music sales in sales.duckdb.

Key insight: an exact-equality join would undercount. Combining all Beyoncé-linked “Get Me Bodied” IDs was necessary for a complete answer: $377.62 + $223.82 = $601.44.
