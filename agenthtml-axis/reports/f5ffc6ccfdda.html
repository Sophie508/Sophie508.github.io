1 Task statement

The Overview sheet already has a date selector in A2 (data validation) and INDEX/MATCH formulas in B2:C2 that pull the two paycheck amounts for that date from the Pay Dates sheet. Bi-weekly total is B4 = SUM(B2:C2).

Goal: when a pay date is chosen in A2, automatically compute the monthly total of all paychecks in that month into Overview!B5 (no separate month selector required). Optionally label the month in A5; the benchmark answer cell is only B5.

Example: If A2 = 2016-01-29, then January pay dates (Jan 1, 15, 29) should sum to 4800 in B5.

4 Solution

Formula written to Overview!B5

Excel formula
=SUMIFS('Pay Dates'!$B:$B,'Pay Dates'!$A:$A,">="&EOMONTH($A$2,-1)+1,'Pay Dates'!$A:$A,"<="&EOMONTH($A$2,0))
+SUMIFS('Pay Dates'!$C:$C,'Pay Dates'!$A:$A,">="&EOMONTH($A$2,-1)+1,'Pay Dates'!$A:$A,"<="&EOMONTH($A$2,0))
  • EOMONTH($A$2,-1)+1 → first day of the month of the selected date
  • EOMONTH($A$2,0) → last day of that month
  • Two SUMIFS add Paycheck 1 and Paycheck 2 for all Pay Dates rows in range
  • Driven solely by A2, so bi-weekly INDEX/MATCH and monthly total stay in sync

solve.py (agent artifact)

from openpyxl import load_workbook


input_path = "/Users/julia/Git/agentHTML/trajs_regen/spreadsheet_office/03_task_8942/workspace/input.xlsx"
output_path = "/Users/julia/Git/agentHTML/trajs_regen/spreadsheet_office/03_task_8942/workspace/output.xlsx"

workbook = load_workbook(input_path)
overview = workbook["Overview"]

# Sum both paycheck columns for every pay date in the month selected in A2.
overview["B5"] = (
    "=SUMIFS('Pay Dates'!$B:$B,'Pay Dates'!$A:$A,\">=\"&EOMONTH($A$2,-1)+1,"
    "'Pay Dates'!$A:$A,\"<=\"&EOMONTH($A$2,0))"
    "+SUMIFS('Pay Dates'!$C:$C,'Pay Dates'!$A:$A,\">=\"&EOMONTH($A$2,-1)+1,"
    "'Pay Dates'!$A:$A,\"<=\"&EOMONTH($A$2,0))"
)

workbook.save(output_path)
print("Overview!B5 =", overview["B5"].value)
