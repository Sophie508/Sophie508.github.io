What Was Done

The strongest evidence is the final checkout snapshot: it contained an order summary for 3 meals for 2 people and a disabled final order button.

Completed setup

The agent reached the payment page with the plan already configured. The case should be read as a near-complete checkout attempt rather than a confirmed subscription.

  • Selected HelloFresh Canada after the initial country mismatch.
  • Verified delivery availability for the dummy user’s postal region.
  • Selected Vegetarian and Plant based preferences.
  • Reached the checkout summary for 3 meals, 2 people, 6 servings.
  • Entered account and delivery form data from provided dummy information.

Completion gap

Payment could not be submitted inside the benchmark browser session. The trajectory records repeated waits, refresh/navigation attempts, iframe inspection, and a forced click attempt, but the payment control stayed disabled.

  • The embedded payment form continued to show a loading state.
  • The button state check returned disabled: true.
  • No successful final order confirmation appears in the trajectory.
