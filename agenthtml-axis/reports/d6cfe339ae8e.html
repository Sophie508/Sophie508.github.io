Technical and pedagogical insights regarding Case 02_t6AbQbAERCs.

Why does the tutor prescribe the simple arithmetic average instead of a weighted average?
In Statistical Quality Control, when subgroups have varying sizes (nᵢ), the Grand Mean must be weighted: X̄̄ = Σ(nᵢ·X̄ᵢ) / Σnᵢ. However, in this case study, every sample has an identical sample size of n = 6. By mathematical invariance, [n·Σ(X̄ᵢ)] / (k·n) = Σ(X̄ᵢ) / k. Identifying this equality is the key conceptual insight that unblocks the student.
What are the subsequent steps required to complete the Shewhart X̄ and R charts?
