Mind2Web Benchmark Quirks & DOM Anomalies

1. Step 13 SVG <path> Unmapped Candidate Quirk

In Step 13, action_reprs[12] records [path] -> CLICK, but pos_candidates in trajectory.json is completely empty ([]). Analysis of the DOM reveals that the user clicked directly on the vector SVG path inside <span backend_node_id="30554"> (the 5-star rating icon). In Mind2Web's HTML cleaning pipeline, leaf SVG paths are frequently pruned or left unindexed as candidates, leaving 0 positive candidates. The surrounding chip container is rooms-chip (backend_node_id 30550) with text "5".

2. Human Demonstrator Temporal Shift (June vs. July)

The original task specification asked for "June 13 to 16". However, in Step 5, the demonstrator explicitly clicked the "Jul" tab (div backend_node_id="8921") and booked July 13 to 16. This is standard in live web datasets when the benchmark recording occurs chronologically after the dates mentioned in the task prompt, forcing the annotator to advance one month to avoid disabled past dates.

3. Negative Candidate Pool Volume

Ryanair Rooms is built on Angular with rich shadow DOM and heavy component trees. During filter steps (Steps 14–20), the negative candidate pool exceeds 1,480 interactive elements per step. Autonomous agents evaluating on Mind2Web must rank the single intended filter among ~1,500 distractors, highlighting the difficulty of this benchmark sample.
