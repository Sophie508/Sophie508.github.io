// The specification

Confidence, measured as distance from uniform

The task frames token-prediction confidence as KL divergence from the uniform distribution U. The goal: a valid distribution P that sits at divergence 10.0 in both directions at once.

The two metrics

Forward KL — confidence of P KL(P∥U) = Σi P(i) · log( P(i) / U(i) ) = 10.0
Backward KL — surprise of U under P KL(U∥P) = Σi U(i) · log( U(i) / P(i) ) = 10.0
Both divergences must land within ±0.001 of 10.0. Higher KL means the model is more confident — its mass has pulled away from the flat uniform baseline.

Constraints

  • Vocabulary size · n150,000
  • Uniform baseline · U(i)1 / n
  • Target KL(P∥U)10.0
  • Target KL(U∥P)10.0
  • Tolerance± 0.001
  • P must be validΣ P = 1, P ≥ 0
◆ /app/dist.npy — NumPy array output
Toolkit provided: numpy + scipy. For n = 150,000, log(n) ≈ 11.918 — leaving little headroom above the target of 10.

Why did it hang? ›
The agent's own diagnosis: "the analytical search loop is taking too long." The solver iterated a search over a 150,000-element distribution while trying to hit two tight ±0.001 targets simultaneously. With the process launched in the background and no incremental stdout, every BashOutput call returned only a bare running status — giving the agent no signal to act on except elapsed time. It waited, re-checked, waited again, and finally cut it off.
