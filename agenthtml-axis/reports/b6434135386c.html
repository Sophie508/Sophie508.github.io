  • Transcript reliabilityThe text is machine transcription and is visibly noisy: repeated phrases (“keep keep keep, keep”), mangled grammar, and content that reads as misrecognition. A literal string count is exactly as trustworthy as the ASR, and this case supplies no audio to re-check against.
  • GranularityTimestamps are per block, not per word. The two hits in block 02 sit somewhere inside 8.6–16.3 s, and the third inside 29.1–35.7 s; the ruler marks ranges, not instants, and does not invent finer precision.
  • Counting riskBecause two of three occurrences fall in one block, a reader who scanned only for “which lines mention it” would land on 2 and pick option B. The correct count requires reading inside the block, which is what the model did.
