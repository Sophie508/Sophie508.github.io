01 / What was requested

Reproduce the mechanism behind alignment.

The paper argues that DPO bypasses toxic capabilities rather than removing them. The task asked for complete, runnable reproduction code, following author clarifications and excluding blacklisted resources. Llama 2 was explicitly out of scope.

01

Locate toxic representations

Train a two-output toxicity probe, rank MLP value vectors against its toxic column, and apply SVD to the corrected d_model × N matrix.

probe.py · vectors.py · Table 1
02

Build preferences & align

Regenerate 24,576 continuation pairs with greedy and PPLM generation. Train full-parameter DPO with a 90:10 split and RMSProp.

pplm.py · dpo.py · configs/paper.yaml
03

Measure & explain the change

Evaluate toxicity, perplexity and token F1; implement residual interventions, toxic-key scaling, activation analysis and PCA.

evaluation.py · analysis.py · cli.py

These are implemented pathways and intended outputs; the record does not establish numerical fidelity. The addendum substitutes unbiased-toxic-roberta for Perspective API, so toxicity scores are not directly interchangeable.
