Faithfulness note: incomplete or unavailable evidence

What this report could not verify, and how the large inputs were read.

  • No post-final-action state. Mind2Web snapshots precede each action; there is no snapshot after step 6, so the final filtered list is unobserved.
  • No screenshots or media. The case contains only text/JSON; no images are embedded because none were supplied.
  • Location was not set in the record. “10001, NY” and the 100-mile radius appear already applied on the results page. Nothing in the six actions sets them; the sign-in and location origin are outside the recorded window.
  • Partial reading of the trajectory HTML. The ~1.0 million characters of cleaned HTML were parsed programmatically, not read verbatim. Complete text content was extracted for the snapshots of steps 1, 2, 3 and 6; for steps 4 and 5 only the filter panel, result header and card counts were extracted, plus a 50-line window around every target. Negative-candidate attributes were sampled (first three per step plus targeted lookups), not read in full.
  • Truncated raw export. In raw_text.jsonl the six action records are upstream-truncated to head/tail excerpts; the complete records were taken from trajectory.json instead.
  • Two oversized tool outputs during evidence loading were spilled to session files that were deliberately not opened; the queries were re-run with tighter bounds.
  • Task spelling. The source task says “Retriver”; the site and the annotator used “Retriever”. This is treated as the same breed.
