Paper Implementation Details

AreaCaptured details
SDE choices VE SDE and VP SDE for benchmarks. VE uses sigma_min = 0.01 for 2D/SIR/Two Moons and 0.05 otherwise; VP uses beta_min = 0.1, beta_max = 11.0.
Network Three MLP embeddings for theta_t and x, sinusoidal t embedding of dimension 64, concatenated into a 3-layer score MLP with 256 hidden units and SiLU activations.
Training Adam at 1e-4, 15% validation split, early stop after 1000 non-improving steps, maximum 3000 training iterations. Batch sizes vary by simulation budget and sequential/non-sequential mode.
Sampling Probability-flow ODE with RK45. TSNPSE estimates high-posterior regions using 20,000 approximate posterior samples and a 5e-4 quantile threshold.
Benchmarks Gaussian Linear, Gaussian Mixture, Two Moons, Gaussian Linear Uniform, Bernoulli GLM, SLCP, SIR, and Lotka Volterra.
Addendum constraints For sequential VESDE, compute sigma_max using only first-round training points. Avoid the blacklisted SNPSE ICML repository.
