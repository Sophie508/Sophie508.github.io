Why the tutor's targeted prescription succeeds where generic tutoring fails
The Subgroup Invariant: Equal Sample Sizes (n = 6)
In Statistical Quality Control, if sample sizes vary across subgroups (e.g. n1 ≠ n2), the overall mean requires a weighted formulation:

Χ̄̄ = (∑ ni Χ̄i) / (∑ ni)

Because the problem explicitly specifies that all 10 samples have size n = 6, each sample carries an equal weight of 1/10. Identifying this condition immediately removes the student's cognitive block without introducing unnecessary algebraic complexity.
Subsequent Steps in the SQC Problem
Once the student completes this key step (Χ̄̄ = 53.90), the remaining workflow follows standard Shewhart procedures:
  • Mean Range: R̄ = ∑ Ri / 10 = 84.0 / 10 = 8.40
  • Factors for n = 6: Standard table gives A2 = 0.483, D3 = 0, D4 = 2.004
  • Χ̄-Chart Limits: UCL = 53.90 + 0.483(8.40) = 57.96; LCL = 53.90 - 0.483(8.40) = 49.84
  • Process Diagnosis: Samples 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10 fall outside the limits → Process is out of statistical control.
